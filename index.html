RESPIN Challenges

Shared tasks on speech technology for Indian languages, from the RESPIN project at SPIRE Lab, IISc Bengaluru.

About RESPIN

RESPIN (Speech Recognition in Agriculture and Finance for the Poor in India) is a project at SPIRE Lab, IISc Bengaluru. Its RESPIN-S1.0 corpus has more than 10,000 hours of read speech in over 38 dialects of nine Indian languages, from the agriculture and finance domains. The challenges below build on RESPIN data.

Challenges

  • MADST 2027

    Status: Upcoming

    IEEE ASLI 2027, Singapore

    MADST: Multilingual Any-Direction Speech Translation Challenge across Nine Indian Languages

    Translate speech between any two of nine Indian languages, into text or directly into speech.

  • MADASR 2.0

    Status: Completed

    ASRU 2025

    MADASR 2.0: Multi-Lingual Multi-Dialect ASR in 8 Indian Languages

    Multilingual ASR across 33 dialects of eight Indian languages: Bengali, Bhojpuri, Chhattisgarhi, Kannada, Magahi, Maithili, Marathi and Telugu.

  • MADASR

    Status: Completed

    ASRU 2023

    MADASR: Model ADaptation for ASR in low-resource Indian languages

    ASR for two low-resource Indian languages, Bengali and Bhojpuri, exploring how much acoustic and text data each matter.
